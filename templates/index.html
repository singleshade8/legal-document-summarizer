Privacy and limitations

Sample judgments translate between English, Hindi, and Tamil offline (built in). For your own documents, cross-language translation uses third-party online services and may send the extracted summary text to that provider. Legal terms may be mistranslated. Chat uses Google Gemini first and falls back to xAI Grok if Gemini times out or fails, when both API keys are configured. Your question and active document text/relevant excerpts are sent to whichever provider handles that request. Do not use chat or cross-language translation for confidential documents unless that transfer is acceptable. Verify important findings, qualifications, and operative directions against the original judgment. Nyaya Saar provides a reading aid, not legal advice, and is not an official government or court service.
